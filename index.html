Domination Game Rules

Game Components

  • Game Board: A world map divided into islands scattered across the board.
  • Resource Cards: Brick, Ore, Wool, Wheat, and Wood.
  • Player Pieces: Armies, upgraded armies, Cities, Villages, Defenses, and Boats.
  • Dice: Players roll for resources each turn.

Setup

  • Each player starts on a chosen island with their color-coded castle.
  • Place all player pieces in a pile near the board.
  • With 2 players, start on opposite diagonal territories.
  • With 3–4 players, players may start anywhere.
  • Begin with 1 of each resource.
  • Organize resource cards into separate piles.

Income

  • Roll one die every turn.
  • Collect 1 resource corresponding to your roll.
  • If you own an island with the matching number, receive 2 resources.
  • If you have a City on the island, receive 1 resource when another player rolls that number.
  • You must have a Village or City on an island to claim its resources.

Resource Types

  • 🪵 Wood
  • 🧱 Brick
  • 🌾 Wheat
  • 🐑 Wool
  • ⛏️ Ore
  • 🃏 Wild — choose any resource

Building

  • Build armies on islands you own, except main islands.
  • Build Villages or Cities on unclaimed islands.
  • You need a Boat to reach a new island, but Boats can be reused.
  • Main islands cannot have defenses or armies.
  • Each main island automatically has +3 defense.

Combat

  • You need a Boat and an Army to attack another island.
  • Attacking a Village gives the defender -2 to their roll.
  • Attacking a City gives the defender -1 to their roll.
  • If an Army is present, both players roll a die.
  • The higher roll wins.
  • A Defense gives the defender +2.
  • Only one Defense is allowed per island.
  • Armies and Defenses together grant the defender +2.
  • A single-upgraded Army gets +1 when attacking.
  • A double-upgraded Army gets +2 when attacking.
  • Upgraded Army bonuses do not apply while defending.
  • In a tie, the attacker wins.
  • If the attacker wins, enemy pieces are returned to the bank.
  • If the attacker loses, their attacking Army is returned to the bank.

Winning

  • Elimination: Conquer all other players.
  • When a player is defeated, take their resource cards and remove their pieces.
  • Conquering: Conquer all islands.

Trading

  • Players may freely negotiate trades with each other.
  • Trades must be mutually agreed upon.
  • Trade 2 of the same resource to the bank for 1 different resource.

Upgrading

  • A single upgrade grants +1 to attack rolls.
  • A double upgrade grants +2 to attack rolls.
  • You can upgrade 3 armies once each.
  • You can upgrade 1 army twice.
  • Upgrades are purchased with resources.
  • Upgrade bonuses only apply when attacking.
